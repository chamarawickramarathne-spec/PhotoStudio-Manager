Account Deletion

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your PhotoStudio Manager account and all associated studio data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open PhotoStudio Manager and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete account.

The account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. The following data is removed:

  • Your account and studio profile
  • Your uploaded logo or profile image
  • All clients
  • All bookings, including wedding details
  • All payment schedules and installments
  • Invoice data derived from those records
  • Scheduled payment reminders on the device

Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email us from the address associated with your account and request account deletion. We will verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email: chamara.wickramarathne@gmail.com
Subject: PhotoStudio Manager account deletion

Data that may be retained

We do not keep a separate copy of your studio data. Encrypted backups maintained by our hosting provider may persist for a short period before they rotate out. Legal or accounting obligations may require retaining limited records; if so, only the minimum required data is kept and access is restricted.
