PhotoStudio Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

PhotoStudio Manager ("the app", "we", "us") is a studio management app for photographers. This policy explains what information the app stores, why it is stored, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

1. Who controls your data

The app is operated by the photographer or studio that installs it. Each account is a single-tenant workspace. The developer, Chamara Wickramarathne, provides the software and does not sell or rent your data.

2. Information the app stores

  • Account details: name, email address, optional phone number, and password (handled by Supabase Auth; we never see your password).
  • Studio profile: business name, contact details, address, website, portfolio link, bio, currency, and an optional logo or profile image.
  • Client records: names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, status, and notes you enter.
  • Booking records: event type, dates, times, locations, package details, amounts, and notes.
  • Payment records: payment schedules, due dates, installments, amounts, payment methods, and notes.
  • Invoice data: invoice previews and PDFs generated on your device from the records above.

3. How information is used

The information is used only to provide the app's features: managing clients, bookings, payment schedules, reminders, invoices, and reports. The app does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

4. Where data is stored

Account and studio data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database with row-level security, so each account can access only its own records. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. A Supabase session token is stored on your device so you stay signed in.

Android payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Invoice PDFs are generated on your device; sharing a PDF is initiated by you through the Android share sheet.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase, as the database and authentication provider.
  • Google Play, for app distribution and updates.
  • Recipients you choose, when you share an invoice PDF using the system share sheet.
  • Authorities, when required by applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains in your account until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete individual clients, bookings, schedules, and installments in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and all related clients, bookings, payment schedules, installments, and stored profile images.

See Account Deletion for the in-app steps and the email request process.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your studio profile and all client, booking, and payment records in the app. You can delete records individually or delete the entire account. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have the right to access, correct, export, or object to processing; contact us to exercise those rights.

8. Children

The app is intended for professional photographers and studio owners. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS in transit, row-level security in the database, and per-account access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; protect your device and account credentials.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated, and material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing.
