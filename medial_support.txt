PhotoStudio Manager

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
--------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is the all-in-one app for photographers to manage clients, bookings, and payments - on your phone and on Windows desktop, with a brand-new revenue dashboard.

3. LONG DESCRIPTION
-------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is a studio management app built for photographers who want to run their business without the paperwork. Keep every client contact in one place, book shoots in seconds, and track payments with confidence - whether you are working from your phone on location or from your desktop in the studio.

Create and manage client profiles with contact details, addresses, and notes. Book any event with dedicated forms - including a complete Wedding booking form with wedding and homecoming details, album preferences, and photo sizes. Track your schedule from pending to completed with a one-tap status stepper and never miss a shoot again.

Manage money effortlessly with payment schedules (deposit, milestone, and final) and record installments as payments come in. The app automatically marks schedules as paid when fully settled and flags overdue amounts, so you always know who owes you and how much.

The redesigned dashboard puts your whole business at a glance: four stat tiles for Total Bookings, Active Clients, Monthly Revenue, and Outstanding, plus an interactive monthly revenue bar chart with 1, 3, 6, and 12-month range selectors and a period total. Recent Bookings and Payments Due keep your schedule and cash flow in view, and one-tap quick actions let you create a booking, add a client, or record a payment in seconds - all with a switchable working currency that adapts to your local rates.

The Windows desktop edition brings the full app to your computer with a resizable window, automatic one-click updates, and the same secure cloud account - every booking, client, and payment stays perfectly synchronized between your phone and your PC.
