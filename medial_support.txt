1. TITLE
--------
PhotoStudio Manager

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
--------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is the all-in-one app for photographers to manage clients, bookings, and payments - on your phone, on Windows desktop, and in your browser. One secure cloud account, an editorial rust-and-gold dashboard, interactive revenue analytics, smart validated forms with native calendar and clock pickers, and layouts that look great on any screen.

3. LONG DESCRIPTION
-------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is a studio management app built for photographers who want to run their business without the paperwork. Keep every client contact in one place, book shoots in seconds, and track payments with confidence - whether you are on location with your phone, in the studio on your Windows desktop, or checking in from any browser.

Create and manage client profiles with contact details, addresses, and notes. Book any event with dedicated forms - including a complete Wedding booking form with wedding and homecoming details, album preferences, and photo sizes. Track your schedule from pending to completed with a one-tap status stepper and never miss a shoot again.

Picking dates and times is effortless everywhere: every date field opens your device's real calendar and every time field opens its clock picker. On Windows and the web you get the browser's native date and time pickers; on Android the app opens the phone's own system calendar and clock dialogs - no typing dates or times by hand, ever.

Manage money effortlessly with payment schedules (deposit, milestone, and final) and record installments as payments come in. The app automatically marks schedules as paid when fully settled and flags overdue amounts, so you always know who owes you and how much. A booking can only ever have one final payment schedule - enforced in the app and in the database - so your books can never double-book the final invoice.

The redesigned dashboard puts your whole business at a glance - a personalized greeting header with your app version, four stat tiles with month-over-month trend badges (Total Bookings, Active Clients, Monthly Revenue, Outstanding) that line up in a single row on desktop, an interactive monthly revenue bar chart with 1/3/6/12-month range selectors, tap-any-bar month readouts and a period total. A "Next Shoots" strip previews your upcoming jobs, and Payments Due groups overdue and pending amounts with progress bars so nothing slips through. Slim one-tap quick actions let you create a booking, add a client, or record a payment in seconds - all with a switchable working currency that adapts to your local rates.

The Windows desktop edition brings the full app to your computer with a resizable window and automatic one-click updates; the Android app now updates itself too - on launch it quietly checks for a newer release and offers a verified one-tap install, so you always run the latest version. The browser edition shares the same instantly available experience. Every booking, client, and payment stays perfectly synchronized across every device through your secure cloud account. Log in once and the app remembers you: your session is saved automatically and restored on every launch, on mobile, desktop, and web alike. Update messages are plain and helpful: if the newest release is not yet available for your exact device, the app tells you so clearly instead of showing a cryptic error.

A dedicated Reports tab turns your data into decisions - monthly revenue trends, collected vs outstanding vs overdue totals, revenue by event type, payment-method breakdowns, top clients by revenue, and a bookings pipeline from pending to completed. Saving is satisfying and clear: every create, edit, status change, or deletion confirms with an instant on-screen toast, bookings open instantly from the list with full details, and you can change a booking's status right from the list card without opening it. Installments can be deleted with one tap and the schedule's paid balance updates immediately, deposit schedules track their own paid-to-date, and fully paid schedules explain why no further installments can be recorded.

Every form is built in a warm editorial style - rust-red primary and gold accents, clear labels above fields, grouped bordered sections with tidy headers, and a right-aligned Cancel/Save action bar. On the desktop app each form type uses a width tuned to its content: the booking form gets a wide, centered column so its two-column and side-by-side grids stay roomy, while client and payment-schedule forms use a more compact centered column, and the record-installment sheet stays small and focused. Mobile keeps the familiar full-width layout. Validation is smarter too: whitespace-only names and phone numbers are rejected, deposit and total amounts are checked so deposits never exceed the total, record-installment entries can't be negative or overpay an outstanding balance, and wedding contexts require a proper due date for payment schedules. Booking, client, payment, and install data can only be saved when every value is valid and sensible - so your books stay clean.

Moving between screens is rock-solid on the web-first engine: a dependencies-level global shim keeps react-native animations safe on every modern browser, so smooth transition fades on the dashboard and revenue chart never throw, whether you are switching tabs on Windows, in your phone browser, or inside the Android app. On Android the hardware back button now works the way you expect - it steps back through your screens, closes any open dialog or menu first, and only asks you to "press back again to exit" when you reach your home screen, so you never lose your place by accident.
