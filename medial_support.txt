PhotoStudio Manager

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
--------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is the all-in-one app for photographers to manage clients, bookings, and payments - on your phone and now on Windows desktop.

3. LONG DESCRIPTION
-------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is a studio management app built for photographers who want to run their business without the paperwork. Keep every client contact in one place, book shoots in seconds, and track payments with confidence - whether you are working from your phone on location or from your desktop in the studio.

Create and manage client profiles with contact details, addresses, and notes. Book any event with dedicated forms - including a complete Wedding booking form with wedding and homecoming details, album preferences, and photo sizes. Track your schedule from pending to completed with a one-tap status stepper and never miss a shoot again.

Manage money effortlessly with payment schedules (deposit, milestone, and final) and record installments as payments come in. The app automatically marks schedules as paid when fully settled and flags overdue amounts, so you always know who owes you and how much. The responsive dashboard shows today's shoots, active bookings, and collected vs outstanding totals in clean, readable summary cards, with one-tap quick actions and a switchable working currency that adapts to your local rates.

The new Windows desktop edition brings the full app to your computer with a resizable window, automatic one-click updates, and the same secure cloud account - every booking, client, and payment stays perfectly synchronized between your phone and your PC.
