1. TITLE
--------
PhotoStudio Manager

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
--------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is the all-in-one app for photographers to manage clients, bookings, and payments - on your phone and on Windows desktop, with an editorial rust-and-gold dashboard, interactive revenue analytics, smart validated forms, and per-form-type layouts that look great on any screen.

3. LONG DESCRIPTION
-------------------
PhotoStudio Manager is a studio management app built for photographers who want to run their business without the paperwork. Keep every client contact in one place, book shoots in seconds, and track payments with confidence - whether you are working from your phone on location or from your desktop in the studio.

Create and manage client profiles with contact details, addresses, and notes. Book any event with dedicated forms - including a complete Wedding booking form with wedding and homecoming details, album preferences, and photo sizes. Track your schedule from pending to completed with a one-tap status stepper and never miss a shoot again.

Manage money effortlessly with payment schedules (deposit, milestone, and final) and record installments as payments come in. The app automatically marks schedules as paid when fully settled and flags overdue amounts, so you always know who owes you and how much.

The redesigned dashboard puts your whole business at a glance - a personalized greeting header with your app version, four stat tiles with month-over-month trend badges (Total Bookings, Active Clients, Monthly Revenue, Outstanding) that line up in a single row on desktop, an interactive monthly revenue bar chart with 1/3/6/12-month range selectors, tap-any-bar month readouts and a period total. A "Next Shoots" strip previews your upcoming jobs, and Payments Due groups overdue and pending amounts with progress bars so nothing slips through. Slim one-tap quick actions let you create a booking, add a client, or record a payment in seconds - all with a switchable working currency that adapts to your local rates.

The Windows desktop edition brings the full app to your computer with a resizable window, automatic one-click updates, and the same secure cloud account - every booking, client, and payment stays perfectly synchronized between your phone and your PC. Log in once and the app remembers you: your session is saved automatically and restored on every launch, on mobile and desktop alike.

Every form is built in a warm editorial style - rust-red primary and gold accents, clear labels above fields, grouped bordered sections with tidy headers, and a right-aligned Cancel/Save action bar. On the desktop app each form type now uses a width tuned to its content: the booking form gets a wide, centered column so its two-column and side-by-side grids stay roomy, while client and payment-schedule forms use a more compact centered column, and the record-installment sheet stays small and focused. Mobile keeps the familiar full-width layout. Validation is smarter too: whitespace-only names and phone numbers are rejected, deposit and total amounts are checked so deposits never exceed the total, record-installment entries can't be negative or overpay an outstanding balance, and wedding contexts require a proper due date for payment schedules. Booking, client, payment, and install data can only be saved when every value is valid and sensible - so your books stay clean.
